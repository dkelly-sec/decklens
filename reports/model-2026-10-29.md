# DeckLens model report (2026-10-29)

Predicts which ranked deck wins a 1v1 battle, from the two decks' cards and card levels only. Player identity is never used.

## Data

* Training: 13,417 battles, 2026-09-01 23:44 UTC to 2026-10-29 23:55 UTC
* Test: 3,355 battles, 2026-10-29 23:55 UTC to 2026-10-30 05:36 UTC (strictly later than all training battles)
* League filter: none
* Cards modelled: 121 (seen at least 30 times in training)
* Test battles won by side A: 47.5% (sides are assigned arbitrarily, so this should be near 50%)

## Results on the test set

|Model|Accuracy (95% range)|Log loss|Brier|
|-|-|-|-|
|Coin flip (always 50%)|50.0% (48.3%–51.7%)|0.6931|0.2500|
|Card levels only|50.2% (48.5%–51.9%)|0.6935|0.2502|
|Logistic regression (card strengths + levels)|50.5% (48.9%–52.2%)|0.6930|0.2499|
|Gradient-boosted trees (card pairs + levels)|52.8% (51.1%–54.5%)|0.6902|0.2485|

Lower log loss and Brier are better. A coin flip scores 0.6931 log loss and 0.25 Brier.

## Limits

* Skill decides many games and isn't modelled, so accuracy has a low ceiling.
* Card weights are average strength in the current meta, not counters; the tree model is the one that can learn card-vs-card interactions.
* Rare cards are left out until they've been seen enough times.
